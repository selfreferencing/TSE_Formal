## Conventions and model boundary

Board counts `n` start at zero; the strict paired sign theorem explicitly requires `0 < n`. Populations and survivor sizes are natural numbers; expressions such as `S - (P - K)` are natural subtraction before casting to real. `rplus`, `rminus`, and `tau` are the three score-order probabilities. The tied quantity `T_A` is a conditional **survivor count**. The pairwise fair-coin win probability is separately `PS.winProbability`. No theorem identifies those two tie mechanisms.

IID observations are functions on distinct coordinates of `Law.pi`; within-board information is encoded by the difference taking values +1, -1, or 0. The implementation of selection sorts actual roster slots by descending score, ascending integer tie key, and original slot index. Its output has exactly S distinct slots. Local relation instances are proved; no global instances are asserted.

The finite `EvolutionState` keeps the ordered roster, bounded evaluation counters, bounded histories, and generation. It is an interface state type. The module does **not** construct the experiment's entire multi-generation transition, prove that this interface is sufficient for every simulator detail, or prove a count-state non-Markov counterexample. `full_state_transition_normalized` is only the normalization closure for two supplied finite kernels on this full state; it is not an implementation or sufficiency theorem.
